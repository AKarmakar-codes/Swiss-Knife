# Prompt for Opus 5: Independent Evaluation of Mode-B Thurstonian Elo Ablation Studies

## Research Context & Mechanics

We are preparing a research paper for AAAI introducing **Elo-Swiss Mode B** (`Model_mechanics/elo_swiss_mode_b.py`), a decoding strategy for Large Language Models that selects candidate reasoning steps via a **Probabilistic Thurstonian Elo Tournament**.

### Pipeline & Match Mechanics
At each decoding step:
1. **Candidate Generation**: The Drafter model samples $N$ candidate reasoning steps.
2. **Reward & Uncertainty Estimation**: A DPO Blade model estimates candidate reward $\mu$. A verifier log-ratio proxy estimates candidate uncertainty $\sigma \approx |r_{\text{blade}} - \frac{1}{\beta}(\log \pi_V - \log \pi_D)|$.
3. **Thurstonian Pairwise Tournament**: Matches between candidate $A$ and candidate $B$ are determined by the Thurstonian Case-V normal CDF:
   $$P(A \text{ beats } B) = \Phi\left(\frac{\mu_A - \mu_B}{\sqrt{\sigma_A^2 + \sigma_B^2 + \varepsilon}}\right)$$
4. **Champion Selection**: The champion is selected via a softmax draw over combined Elo rating and blade reward logits:
   $$\text{logit}_i = w_{\text{tournament}} \cdot \frac{R_i - 1500}{T} + w_{\text{blade}} \cdot (\mu_i - \lambda \cdot \sigma_i)$$
5. **Unconditional Acceptance**: Mode B accepts the champion step unconditionally without a verifier threshold gate or fallback resampling.

## Objective Function Definition

The primary target metric is the Tribunal Judge **Scalar Objective ($S_{\text{obj}}$)**, defined as the harmonic mean of Quality and Safety axes:
$$S_{\text{obj}} = \frac{2 \cdot \text{Quality} \cdot \text{Safety}}{\text{Quality} + \text{Safety}}$$
where:
$$\text{Quality} = \text{mean}(\text{response\_quality}, \text{relevance})$$
$$\text{Safety} = 1 - \text{mean}(\text{toxicity}, \text{harmfulness})$$

## Two Primary Ablations to Evaluate

We conducted two primary ablation studies:

### 1. Test 1: Log-Proxy Sigma Validity (`evaluation/test_sigma_validity.py`)
Compares three $\sigma$ conditions:
- **`real_sigma`**: Genuine log-ratio proxy $\sigma$ driving Thurstonian matches.
- **`shuffled_sigma`**: Permuted $\sigma$ assignments (same distribution, randomized candidate assignment).
- **`zero_sigma`**: $\sigma = 0$ for all candidates (collapses matches to standard Bradley-Terry Elo).

**Core Question for Test 1**: Does log-ratio proxy $\sigma$ measure real epistemic/aleatoric uncertainty, or is it merely functioning as random noise?

### 2. Test 2: Thurstonian Tournament Value (`evaluation/test_tournament_value.py`)
Compares three candidate selection strategies:
- **`thurstonian`**: Probabilistic Thurstonian Elo tournament ($w_{\text{tournament}} > 0, \text{probabilistic}=\text{True}$).
- **`elo_baseline`**: Pure deterministic Elo sorting ($w_{\text{blade}}=0, \text{probabilistic}=\text{False}$).
- **`softmax_blade`**: Direct softmax over raw blade rewards ($w_{\text{tournament}}=0$).

**Core Question for Test 2**: Does the probabilistic Thurstonian tournament add value over deterministic matchups or direct softmax selection?
