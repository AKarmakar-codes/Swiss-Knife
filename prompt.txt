### Key Analytical Deliverables

#### 1. Justifying the Tournament Selection Formulation
* **Thurstonian Elo vs. Elo Baseline (`w_blade=0`) & Softmax Blade (`w_tournament=0`)**: Evaluate win rates and scalar quality gains ($\Delta Q$). Why does tournament ranking outperform direct Softmax sampling? Does it prevent refusal spikes?
* **Disagreement & Reward Gap ($\Delta\mu$) Analysis**: Compare performance on prompts where Thurstonian and Bradley-Terry selection disagreed (`t_bt_disagreement_rate > 0`) vs. agreed. Assess Spearman correlation ($r$) between top-2 candidate reward gap ($\Delta\mu$) and $\Delta Q$.

#### 2. Justifying the Min-Entropy Sigma ($\sigma$) Formulation
* **Uncertainty Tier Breakdown**: Analyze gains ($\Delta Q$) across High $\sigma$, Medium $\sigma$, and Low $\sigma$ tiers. Does Real $\sigma$ yield maximum boost under high uncertainty?
* **Real $\sigma$ vs. Controls**: Contrast Real $\sigma$ against Shuffled $\sigma$ (spatial permutation check) and Zero $\sigma$ (Bradley-Terry zero-variance limit).
* **Diagnostic Correlations**: Correlate $\Delta Q$ with `upset_rate`, `mean_sigma_spread`, and `mean_champion_sigma_rank`.

#### 3. Category, Rubric, & Subset Tradeoffs
* **Pre-Registered Safety Categories**: Breakdown performance across `direct_violence`, `self_harm`, `social_harm`, `deception_manipulation`, `dual_use_technical`, and `benign_informational`.
* **Rubric Tradeoffs**: Analyze how Swiss-Knife balances `quality`, `helpfulness`, `toxicity`, `harmfulness`, and `refusal`.
* **Confident Subset Profile**: What specific prompt characteristics ($\Delta\mu, \sigma$, category) define the top quartile ($\text{Q75}$) gains?

#### 4. Paper-Ready Outputs
* **LaTeX Tables**: Format two publication-ready LaTeX tables for Test 1 ($\sigma$ validity across uncertainty tiers) and Test 2 (tournament value across safety categories).
* **Paper Discussion & Limitations**: Provide concise summary bullet points for the paper abstract/intro and honest failure-mode analysis for the Limitations section.
